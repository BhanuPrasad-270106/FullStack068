About ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is an engineering college located in Sangivalasa, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. The institution focuses on providing quality technical education and developing students' technical and professional skills.

ANITS offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology. The college provides facilities such as laboratories, library, computer facilities, sports and other student activities.

Engineering students study subjects involving formulas such as H2O and mathematical expressions such as x2.

Major Departments

  1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  2. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML)
  3. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  4. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
